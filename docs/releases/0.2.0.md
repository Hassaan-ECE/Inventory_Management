# Inventory Management v0.2.0

- **One local database.** The first time 0.2.0 starts, it combines this computer's three inventory files into one (`inventory-management.feox`). Every record is checked against its original before the switch, and the old files are kept in `pre-0.2-databases\` next to it. If the combine step fails, the old files are left exactly as they were and the app stops instead of opening; tell the app owner.
- **Problems show in the footer.** An amber note appears when shared files were skipped, when a teammate runs a newer version, when the local database is nearly full, or when changes are not being saved on this computer. It stays until the problem clears.
- The unused ME Storage placeholder and TE's placeholder HTML export item are gone from the menus.
- Shared folders on S: and their file formats are unchanged, so computers still on 0.1.4 keep syncing with 0.2.0. They show "Click Update" and stop publishing TE and EVCS Storage snapshots until they update.

Under the hood, the three inventories now share one storage layer, TE and EVCS Storage share one sync engine, and the app's bridge types are generated from the backend, which removes about 24,000 lines (roughly a third of the code) without changing what you see.

Existing databases, shared folders, and the updater signing identity are preserved. Everyone should update to 0.2.0 using the in-app Update button.
